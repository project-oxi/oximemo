Oximemo normalizes spelling to NFC and quantizes each row to signed 8-bit.
The small vocabulary includes 20,000 frequent Hangul entries and 56
additional writing terms. The medium and large editions append writing
terms found in deeper prefixes, then fill out their remaining capacity
in frequency order. Each manifest contains one UTF-8 entry per line.
The app downloads a verified source prefix and stores only the derived
vectors in app-local data. Searches run locally by cosine similarity.
